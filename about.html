Our Story

BUILT ON SNOW, TRUST & THREE DECADES OF SERVICE

For more than 30 years, JP Tadduni, LLC has been the name Stratford, CT homeowners and businesses trust when winter weather hits hard. We're a locally owned, fully insured team that takes every storm seriously — so you don't have to.

Our fleet of trucks, loaders, and skid steers stands ready around the clock. From small residential driveways to large commercial parking lots, our team has the equipment and expertise to keep your property safe, accessible, and clear all winter long.

Serving Stratford, Bridgeport, Trumbull, Shelton, Milford, Fairfield, and surrounding Connecticut towns, we monitor weather conditions around the clock during the winter season so we're always prepared to respond quickly when you need us most.
