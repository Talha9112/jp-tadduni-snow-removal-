Fleet & Equipment

We invest in reliable, well-maintained equipment so we can handle any storm that hits Connecticut.

Full-size commercial plow trucks with heavy-duty blades for clearing large lots, roads, and driveways efficiently.

Sanding & Salting Trucks

Dedicated salt and sand spreaders to treat surfaces before, during, and after winter weather events.

Skid Steers

Compact and powerful machines for clearing tight spaces, stacking snow, and detailed cleanup work.
